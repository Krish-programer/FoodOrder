At FoodOrder, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your details when you use our website and delivery service.

1. Information We Collect

This local demo has no account, order, payment, or message-delivery service. Cart and promo-preview state are stored in this browser. Form values are used only for local validation and are not transmitted by this project.

  • Cart data: Selected dish IDs, quantities, and a demo promo-preview code in browser local storage.
  • Form data: Checkout and contact fields are validated in the browser only; no service receives them.
  • Payment information: No card or bank details are requested or processed.

2. How We Use Your Information

The current demo uses browser storage to keep cart selections and displays local checkout previews. It does not:

  • submit orders or send tracking/status updates;
  • deliver contact messages or operate customer support;
  • process payments or verify promotional eligibility.

3. Cookies & Tracking Technologies

This demo uses browser local storage to remember cart contents and a demo promo-preview code. Sign-in and analytics features are not configured. You can clear local storage in your browser to remove these saved values.

4. Third-Party Services

No payment processor, order service, messaging provider, or delivery-routing integration is configured in this project. The demo does not transmit order, payment, or contact form data to those services.

5. Data Security

This local demo does not provide a production security guarantee. The operator must configure and document appropriate hosting, transport security, access controls, and retention practices before collecting customer data.

6. Your Rights & Choices

Depending on your location, you have the right to access, rectify, or delete the personal data we hold about you. You can also opt out of receiving promotional emails at any time by contacting our support team or updating your account preferences.
